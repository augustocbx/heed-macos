# Issue 69 retrieval acceptance evidence

## Measured retrieval and optional embeddings

The frozen lexical benchmark `ee3f558625b5ab1b30e096e556770b2d4b024dfe` ran five query iterations at 1, 100 and 1,000 authored sources independently on a MacBook Air M1/16 GiB and MacBook Pro M4 Pro/48 GiB, macOS 27.0.1/Bun 1.4.2. Nine positive EN/PT queries and four negative/excluded-only queries cover between-sample facts, paraphrases, names, contradictory commitments, dated tentative updates and label any/all. Empty scopes in the one-source cohort are treated as additional negatives, not invented positives. All eligible ground-truth evidence/source recall at 8 and 16 was 1.0 for lexical retrieval in this deliberately small authored cohort. Negative/excluded-only queries returned no evidence and invoked no generator. Old sampling missed both between-sample facts; at 1,000 sources its PT paraphrase/dated evidence recall was 0.5 and most other positives were zero. These are fixture results, not a library-wide recall guarantee.

## Actual installed-model output quality

An explicit installed `gemma4:e4b` run used the production generator contract at `1f5439bc99b036a29e98c79da1dd9878d8a74d3e` on the M1 Air. Model digest was `dc35e8d9c6061baa6f0fa870975ab6932e2542b579b13ea0f199fa4bb7300c9c` (6,583,656,505 bytes, 7.5B Q4_K_M). It used existing loopback Ollama, no downloads or installed-setting changes, and `keep_alive=0`. All 13 questions completed; all four negative/excluded-only questions made zero calls and returned zero claims. Nine positives generated eight claims across two questions; the other seven returned empty answers despite their ground-truth evidence being supplied to the model. The EN between-sample answer retained both confirmed and tentative release statements and rejected-Friday/confirmed-Tuesday wording. The PT paraphrase answer distinguished the approved two-thousand-real budget from the earlier unapproved one-thousand-real proposal. Manual review of every generated claim found its quoted support and no invented name/date/commitment in these eight claims; this small cohort is not a universal accuracy guarantee.

All 27 actual calls respected the 5,500-byte system-plus-JSON budget (maximum measured 5,481 bytes), 8,192-token context and four-call cap. Model outputs, per-query supplied IDs, partial reasons and failures are recorded separately from retrieval recall. Empty positive answers demonstrate imperfect generation recall, even with valid retrieved evidence; user-facing source inspection and partial coverage remain necessary. Concurrent synthetic verification ran during part of this semantic-output evaluation, so its wall times are not an idle model-performance benchmark. The public fixture store was removed and the QA process exited; no model remained admitted after completion.

## Storage lifecycle regression

An initial production-route failure exposed a completed search index still counted as protected after its rebuild reservation reached zero. A valid smaller quota was permanently rejected; this was not treated as a startup race or dismissed as test contention. The correction explicitly admits the lifecycle-owned, idle search generation as one disposable bundle, includes eligibility in the review token and retires that complete bundle before considering eligible media. Public review separates aggregate rebuildable-cache bytes/file count from its media-only removal list.

The settled-index regression requests one byte below total usage, requiring the whole cache to be reviewed even though the pointer alone would satisfy that reduction. Preview preserves the index; Confirm closes owned SQLite connections and removes only the reviewed files. Accepted source bytes, revisions and all WAV bytes remain unchanged. Subsequent current-source chat uses bounded fallback, larger quotas allow rebuild, and small quotas persist safely across restart. Held reader/writer leases, foreign reservations, missing members, hardlinks, symlinks and unrelated provider files are covered independently. An external review also reproduced a missing-active-database edge; eligibility now requires the database member rather than accepting a pointer-only bundle. ID-cache invalidation precedes retirement, including its exceptional paths.
