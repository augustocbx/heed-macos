# Physical remote metadata deletion

Remote deletion is separate from local meeting deletion, cache eviction and disconnecting a destination. It requires an explicitly selected version set, a current destination preview and a second confirmation. Local transcripts, tasks, chat and audio are preserved.

Create a **new empty v2 library** in the SMB or iCloud connection settings to use this feature. Existing v1 libraries remain readable and writable using their original protocol; they are never converted in place. Older applications reject a v2 header. A writable SMB connection is required for coordinated v2 reads as well as writes.

The storage settings show the exact versions, destination, estimated metadata bytes to remove and audio bytes retained. Confirming removes the selected commit markers, meeting payloads and original manifest contents. A small typed descriptor replaces each canonical manifest to preserve ancestry and prevent publication of the same revision. To publish the preserved local meeting again, explicitly create a fresh revision.

SMB publication, imports, audio downloads and deletion use a persistent exclusive-create destination claim. An interrupted operation can block synchronization on another Mac. The recovery panel exposes the original operation ID; reconciliation requires its original private journal, physical Mac, app-directory identity and guard inode. Copied or restored journals do not authorize takeover. There is no expiry, forced unlock or automatic claim stealing. Local recording can continue after optional transfer cleanup completes.

An incomplete confirmed deletion retries only its original exact targets. SMB results remain **pending cross-device verification**. Real share contention, reconnect and cache behavior require separate mounted two-Mac acceptance.

iCloud removes exact metadata within local file coordinator accessors and remains **pending propagation**. The original device can explicitly reconcile the same job; discovery and synchronization also reconcile one matching private confirmed job per operation. Late replicas are removed only if they match the original confirmed hashes. A deletion record learned from another device hides that version but does not grant new destructive authority. An account, folder or connection-generation change never silently rebinds a job.

**Shared audio garbage collection is disabled in production.** Neither source tests nor a directory scan establish cross-host freshness. Content-addressed media is retained, including shared objects and objects with unresolved references. These statuses do not certify global erasure or a provider durability receipt. Google Drive and OneDrive remain disabled under issue #56.

Private previews and jobs have bounded journals. Failed persistence, malformed controls, changed content, insufficient quota, unknown ownership or inaccessible scopes stop the operation. Preserve the destination and its private state for recovery; do not delete claim files manually.

The private deletion journal is deliberately bounded to 128 confirmed jobs and 64 active previews. Pending iCloud propagation jobs retain their exact original destructive authority for late-replica reconciliation. The application refuses additional jobs at the bound; it does not automatically archive or discard these receipts. Preserve the journal if this limit is reached.

Original interrupted deletion reviews with no confirmed destructive job can expose a release-only recovery action. It performs no new discovery, publication, or deletion; the native guardian must independently verify the original origin and a safe checkpoint before releasing. Configuration changes keep old jobs visible but blocked. A display-name change alone does not change the iCloud binding.

A completed local-only iCloud publication is subsequently observed, not published again. A locally committed import of a fully validated v2 bundle can also be observed in its exact provider/destination/generation scope. Observation confirms and reads the exact marker, manifest and payload; it grants no canonical admission authority and never retires another origin’s pending intent. An uncommitted preview does not grant this status. An interrupted canonical admission may resume only using its device-private pre-rename receipt and original staged/installed inode. Equal bytes from a different inode, copied receipt, new configuration, or unproved stage never grant admission authority.

An original publication whose local source is missing or unusable can expose a native-gated release-only action. This verifies original admission receipts and releases coordination; it never republishes, adopts replicas, or deletes residual payload/audio/pending intents. Such uncommitted residue is retained and is not discoverable through committed revision deletion.

iCloud observes at most eight completed current revision heads per synchronization pass using a durable round-robin cursor. Completed superseded jobs leave the active observation queue; they retain the library's pending copy protection and immutable history. A failed individual source or publication is reported and does not block other jobs. The active job ledger remains bounded to 10,000 jobs. Private original admission receipts are bounded to 20,000 directory entries; they are preserved rather than automatically discarded when the bound is reached.

SMB transaction startup is bounded to 120 seconds. Each RPC has a separate bytes-scaled total deadline of at most 30 minutes (inventory: 10 minutes), plus a 120-second inactivity bound. Serialized native liveness frames do not remove the independent total deadline. Preemption and helper release use bounded cleanup, approximately three seconds. Unsupported native volume capabilities refuse v2 header/claim writes; local guard provisioning may occur, but a fresh refused attempt leaves no recovery journal. Existing v1 read-only imports remain available.
